HOW IT WORKS

From click to decision in milliseconds

Four layers, one verdict. Every score is explained, every action is auditable.

01

Ingest

Click events arrive via HTTP or batch. Server strips client-supplied provenance.

02

Score

Rules, ML (NN+GBT), anomaly (IsolationForest), graph (shared device/IP) and online learning each contribute.

03

Decide

Weighted fusion produces a risk score and an action: allow, monitor, flag, block or reject.

04

Investigate

Analysts triage alerts and incidents, inspect evidence, and approve actions with full audit trails.

ANALYST WORKFLOW

Signal to action, without losing the thread

06

TRUST & OPERATING MODES

Honest about what is real and what is demo

AegisClick runs locally by default. Integrations are optional. Labels on simulated traffic are ground-truth for evaluation, not real fraud.

  • Local-first. SQLite by default. PostgreSQL, Redis and Kafka are optional via environment variables.
  • Simulated traffic. The simulator generates labeled synthetic clicks for testing and evaluation. Labels are ground truth for benchmarking, not evidence of real fraud.
  • AI is advisory. The LLM investigation agent is optional and never executes actions without human approval.
  • No fake proof. No customer logos, testimonials or invented metrics. Detection quality is measured on held-out data.
  • RBAC. Viewer (read), Analyst (operate), Admin (settings/destructive).
  • Audit trail. Every block, unblock, reset and settings change is logged.
  • Model registry. Versioned models with retrain/rollback and PSI drift monitoring.
  • Open source. MIT licensed. Run locally, inspect everything.
